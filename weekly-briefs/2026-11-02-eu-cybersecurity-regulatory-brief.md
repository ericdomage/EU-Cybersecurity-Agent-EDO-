# EU Cybersecurity Regulatory Brief — Week ending 2 November 2026

*Scan covers 27 October – 2 November 2026. Prior-week baseline (26 October 2026) is reused where no new material surfaced. Labels: **In force** / **Transposing** / **Proposed** / **Guidance**.*

---

## Executive summary

- **Council COREPER's 21 October Digital Omnibus mandate crystallised this week** as the dominant signal on incident-reporting architecture: national entry points in each Member State replace the Commission's proposed EU single entry point; ENISA's EU information point is allowed only as a signpost, barred from receiving or storing notifications ([Consilium, 21 Oct 2026](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/10/21/digital-rulebook-council-agrees-position-to-simplify-data-protection-privacy-and-cyber-reporting-rules/)).
- **Telecom security authorities (ECASEC + NIS CG Telecoms/5G workstream) met in Athens on 20–21 October**, publicised 27 October, advancing a single NIS2 incident-reporting template, cross-border supervision guidelines, and progress on the Nevers 5G risk-assessment recommendations ([ENISA, 27 Oct 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/european-union-agency-for-cybersecurity-enisa_enisa-nis2-activity-7520799094638850049-4xDH)).
- **ENISA–EC "Product Cybersecurity: Making CRA a Reality" conference announced 26 October** sets the next set-piece on CRA implementation, with security-by-design and AI-supported compliance on the agenda.
- **NIS2 transposition map worsens in Spain**: Royal Decree 806/2026 (BOE, 6 Oct) dissolved parliament for 29 November elections; the Spanish Law on Coordination and Governance of Cybersecurity will not be adopted before the new chambers meet on 23 December ([Dazr tracker, 29 Oct 2026](https://compliance.dazr.eu/news/nis2-transposition-october-2026)). Ireland, France, Netherlands and Spain remain before the CJEU on the 8 July 2026 infringement.
- **Commission briefing of 28 October**: all time-barred AI Act requests for information have been returned by the >30 providers in scope on model safety/security and copyright; assessments continue, no compliance findings yet ([2eu.brussels](https://2eu.brussels/en/news/european-commission-receives-ai-companies-responses-to-requests-whose-deadlines-have-passed-and-assesses-submissions)).
- **ENISA 2027 NIS360 data-collection survey closed 30 October**, feeding the next biennial maturity assessment for Annex I entities.
- **PQC: national roadmap deadline (31 Dec 2026) is now <60 days out.** No new EU-level publication this week; Germany's 12 October BSI/BMDS/BMI joint guideline remains the most recent national benchmark.
- **CSA2 and the targeted NIS2 amendments stay pre-vote in ITRE**: ENISA Executive Director Juhan Lepassaar will present the State of Cybersecurity in the Union to ITRE on **10 November**, the next political window.

## Per instrument

### 1. NIS2 Directive — **In force / Transposing**

**Discussion.** No new EU-level development on the base directive; political focus is on the targeted amendments (COM(2026)13) and CSA2 package. Dazr's EU-wide transposition tracker, refreshed 29 October, now flags **Spain's parliament dissolved** by RD 806/2026 of 6 October — pushing the Spanish NIS2 law beyond the 23 December reconvening ([compliance.dazr.eu](https://compliance.dazr.eu/news/nis2-transposition-october-2026)). The 20–21 October ECASEC and NIS CG Telecoms/5G meeting addressed Nevers 5G high-risk-vendor progress, cross-border supervision guideline drafting, and a **single NIS2 incident-reporting template** — structurally relevant to Omnibus VII negotiations. NIS CG working-group meetings 4–6 November; full plenary 26 November under the Irish Presidency.

**Implementation agenda.**
- **CJEU infringement proceedings**: Ireland, Spain, France and the Netherlands remain referred (8 July 2026), with lump-sum and daily penalty actions pending.
- **Germany**: BSI enforcement initiative starts Q4 2026 (hearings for unregistered entities), individual audits from Q1 2027. 20,373 registrations by 30 September 2026 vs. ~30,000 expected ([sightadel.com, 9 Oct 2026](https://www.sightadel.com/en/news/nis2-bsi-announces-enforcement-and-first-audits/)). No new BSI action this week.
- Applied national laws: **Austria** NISG 2026 (1 Oct 2026), **Netherlands** Cbw (15 Aug 2026), **Italy** D.Lgs. 138/2024 (16 Oct 2024), **Germany** NIS2UmsuCG (6 Dec 2025). Unchanged.
- **Romania DNSC** first NIS2 sanction (~€10,000, 29 Sep 2026) remains the only primary-sourced fine on the [Nisura enforcement tracker](https://nisura.eu/nis2-enforcement-tracker).

**Organizational impact.**
- Essential/important entities in late-transposing Member States (FR, ES, IE) must still prepare against Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2690 Art. 21 measures — buyers and prime contractors already demand them.
- Entities with German establishments: budget for Q1 2027 audits — § 61(1) BSIG audits need no waiting period. Fines up to €500k for registration offences, €7M for important-entity security-measure breaches.
- Telecoms (ECASEC-regulated): expect ENISA mutual-assistance and cross-border-supervision guidance to firm up at the 26 November plenary.

### 2. DORA — **In force**

**Discussion.** No new ESA joint publication this week. The 16 September 2026 ESAs "DORA Incident Reporting – Operational Instructions" ([ESMA PDF](https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2026-09/DORA_Incident_reporting_-_Operational_instructions.pdf); [EBA mirror](https://eba.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2026-09/3039fc09-0b6e-4468-99bb-e27ae88bcf85/DORA%20IR%20-%20Operational%20Instructions_for%20publication%20%281%29.pdf)) remain controlling: English-language CA-to-ESA templates, monetary fields in thousands, no "N/A" placeholders, corrected intermediate reports where applicable. The ESAs' **first full oversight cycle for the 19 CTPPs** designated 18 November 2025 continues under EBA as Lead Overseer ([JC 2026/10 Annual Report 2025](https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2026-04/JC_2026_10_Joint_Committee_Annual_Report_2025.pdf); [JC 2025/29 Guide](https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2025-07/JC_2025_29__DORA_Guide_on_oversight_activities.pdf)).

**Implementation agenda.**
- Register of information acceptance remains **94% for 2026** (vs. 40% in 2025). Next CTPP annual designation will re-apply quantitative/qualitative criteria; opt-in applications remain open.
- Reporting clocks unchanged: initial notification within 4 hours of classification and ≤24 hours after awareness (RTS (EU) 2025/301 Art. 5(1)(a)); intermediate ≤72 hours; final ≤1 month.
- No new DORA delegated or implementing act in the Official Journal this week.

**Organizational impact.**
- Financial entities: finalise ICT third-party register updates ahead of the next annual CTPP refresh; expect the first wave of **CTPP recommendations** to flow back via competent authorities.
- ICT providers near designation thresholds: factor the opt-in cost and six-month assessment into planning.
- Dual-regulated entities should align DORA pipelines with the forthcoming Omnibus VII national-entry-point architecture; English templates remain the ESAs expectation regardless of local-language free-text.

### 3. eIDAS 2.0 / EUDI Wallet — **In force / Transposing**

**Discussion.** No new ARF release since **ARF v3.0.0 (23 July 2026)**, which aligns with amending CIRs 2024/2977, 2024/2979, 2024/2980, 2024/2982 and 2025/848, and introduces Relying-Party Services, wallet-to-wallet changes, dual Trusted-List/LoTE trust-anchor retrieval and the Functional Conformance Assessment Framework ([GitHub release](https://github.com/eu-digital-identity-wallet/eudi-doc-architecture-and-reference-framework/releases/tag/v3.0.0)). **Topic AA (SCA refinement) iteration 7** (21 Oct – 16 Dec 2026) continues; paper v0.8 published 14 October 2026. New Topics AD (user binding in proximity flows) and AE (liveness in remote flows) opened the same iteration ([ARF discussion topics index](https://atlas.credimi.io/documents/EUDI-ARF-DISCUSSION-TOPICS/)).

**Implementation agenda.** Member States should have at least one EUDI Wallet available for citizens by November 2026 under eIDAS 2.0, but no public rollout milestone was certified this week — watch the Cooperation Group plenary 26 November. **ETSI EN 319 487 (draft) — EUDI Wallet Trust Mark** remains at draft stage (19 October 2026). July 2026 amending CIRs (2026/1730, 2026/1731, 2026/1735) remain in force.

**Organizational impact.** Relying parties building SCA, remote identification or qualified e-signature flows should track Topic AA v0.8 as emerging requirements before iteration closes 16 December. QTSPs: no new obligations this week, but LoTE support is now normative in ARF. Wallet providers: use FCAF (ARF Section 7.5) and conformance.eudi.dev test cases for certification readiness.

### 4. Cybersecurity Act 2 (CSA2) — **Proposed**

**Discussion.** CSA2 (COM(2026)11 / 2026/0011(COD)) remains in ITRE, Markéta Gregorová (Greens/EFA, CZ) rapporteur. **ITRE amendments PE796.470, 796.484, 796.485 and 796.501 tabled 13 October 2026** (45 MEPs on Articles 2, 100, 103). No committee vote scheduled; expected window remains February 2027 per secondary reporting ([epvotes.com dossier](https://epvotes.com/dossiers/2026-0011-european-union-agency-cybersecurity-enisa-european), checked 2 Nov 2026). **ITRE 10 November meeting**: Lepassaar presents the State of Cybersecurity in the Union — a political window for Members to pre-position on CSA2 and the NIS2 amendments ([EP ITRE highlights](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/committees/en/itre/home/highlights)). Council working-party meetings continue; several WK notes remain non-public. Gregorová's core proposals (8 October, [AGENCE EUROPE #13955](https://agenceurope.eu/en/bulletin/article/13955/9/rapporteur-on-cybersecurity-act-2-proposes-mandatory-certification-by-default-to-meps)): mandatory certification by default; cyber-posture scheme for NIS2 essentials within 12 months; **individual** rather than country-based high-risk-supplier assessment; new Annex 2A priority ICT assets (inverters, smart meters, home routers, port cranes, medical imaging); single reporting entry point; Member-State compensation schemes for forced phase-outs.

**Implementation agenda.** No new comitology / delegated-act movement. Existing EUCC/EUCS certification work continues; **ACM v3** (EUCC cryptography) public review closed end-July 2026, no adoption published this week ([EUCC Guidelines on Cryptography](https://certification.enisa.europa.eu/publications/eucc-guidelines-cryptography_en)); applicable ACM remains **v2 (6 May 2025)**.

**Organizational impact.** NIS2-essentials should start budgeting for a cyber-posture scheme deliverable within 12 months of CSA2 entry into force. ICT vendors in Annex 2A categories should prepare individualised supplier dossiers (source-code transparency, independent audits, vulnerability handling) — Gregorová's draft treats these as risk-reducing criteria. NCCAs and certification labs should size resourcing for a materially larger EUCC/EUCS pipeline if mandatory-by-default is adopted.

### 5. Quantum / Post-Quantum Cryptography — **Guidance**

**Discussion.** No new EU-level PQC publication this week. The Commission's July 2026 Cybersecurity-and-AI action plan continues to frame PQC as a cross-cutting objective ([DG CNECT PQC page](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/post-quantum-cryptography)). **Germany's BSI/BMDS/BMI joint PQC implementation guideline of 12 October 2026** remains the most recent high-visibility national benchmark ([BSI publication](https://www.bsi.bund.de/SharedDocs/Downloads/DE/BSI/Krypto/Leitlinie-Umstellung-PQC.html)); France (ANSSI) and the Netherlands (NCSC) maintain pre-existing roadmaps.

**Implementation agenda.**
- **31 December 2026 — Member State national PQC roadmaps due** (NIS CG Coordinated Implementation Roadmap, June 2025). Deadline <60 days away; late Member States should move now.
- 21 December 2030 — high-risk EU use-case migration to PQC.
- 31 December 2035 — full migration.
- ENISA's 30 April 2026 report on hybridisation of traditional cryptography with PQC remains the Agency's practical reference. ACM v3 still awaits adoption; applicable version is ACM v2.

**Organizational impact.** CRA-regulated manufacturers: the Roadmap explicitly hooks into the CRA — PQC-safe software/firmware upgrades enabled by default by 2030 is now a lifecycle expectation. CISOs/CTOs in critical infrastructure (water, energy, health, finance, transport): inventory asymmetric cryptography, prioritise ML-KEM (FIPS 203) for key establishment, plan hybrid deployments, treat "store-now-decrypt-later" as a current threat. Procurement: write crypto-agility and PQC-readiness clauses into contracts whose assets operate past 2030.

## Cross-cutting themes

- **Incident reporting is converging but splintering by design.** The Council's Omnibus VII mandate locks in a federated architecture (national entry points + ENISA signpost) rather than a true EU single desk. For regulated entities reporting under NIS2, DORA, CRA Art. 14, GDPR Art. 33 and e-Privacy, the operational test is local: can your runbook route in parallel to the correct national competent authorities. Parliament's position is still outstanding; trilogues expected Q1–Q2 2027.
- **DORA–NIS2 overlap is still managed by taxonomy alignment, not consolidation.** DORA ICT-related reporting (ITS 2025/302 / RTS 2025/301) and NIS2 single-template work (NIS CG Telecoms/5G workstream) retain separate pipelines; dual-regulated financial entities must map CSIRTs vs. financial competent authorities per incident type.
- **Harmonisation gaps are now political, not technical.** CJEU infringement against IE/ES/FR/NL; Spain's electoral timetable pushing further delay; Austria/Netherlands only recently operational — dispersed transposition is the dominant risk for pan-EU operators planning uniform compliance programmes.
- **Supply-chain focus intensifies.** CSA2 (Annex 2A + mandatory individual high-risk supplier assessment), NIS2 Art. 21 supply-chain measures, CRA security-by-design, and DORA CTPP oversight are converging on a de facto EU supply-chain security stack. Expect the Commission's horizontal supply-chain framework (flagged in ENISA's 2024 State of Cybersecurity in the Union recommendations) to re-surface after Lepassaar's 10 November ITRE presentation.
- **PQC and crypto-agility are now cross-cutting**: CRA (default-safe upgrade capability), eIDAS 2.0 (Trusted/LoTE algorithm stacks), DORA (ICT risk management of cryptographic estate) and EUCC/EUCS (ACM alignment) all depend on the same migration curve now hitting its first binding milestone (31 Dec 2026).
