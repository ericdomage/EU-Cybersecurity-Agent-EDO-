(In force / Guidance) ([ENISA SRP page](https://www.enisa.europa.eu/topics/product-security/vulnerability-services/eu-incident-response-and-cyber-crisis-management/single-reporting-platform-srp))

Expected emphasis: AI-driven threats and lessons from the refrigeration-systems wave. Report not yet in the public domain. (Guidance, pending) ([ITRE notice](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/committees/en/itre/home/highlights))

## Per instrument

### 1. NIS2 Directive (In force / Transposing)

**Discussion.** No substantive EU-level legislative movement this week. The two Commission amendment vehicles that touch NIS2 — COM(2026) 13 of 20 Jan 2026 (companion to CSA2) and the Digital Omnibus COM(2025) 837 of 19 Nov 2025 — remain pending in Parliament (ITRE and LIBE "awaiting committee decision"); the Council's Digital Omnibus mandate agreed by COREPER on 21 Oct 2026 stands as published in Council document 14756/26 of 22 Oct. ([nis2guide.net](https://nis2guide.net/directive/amendments/), [Council mandate summary](https://regreportingdesk.com/digital-omnibus-incident-reporting-national-entry-point/)).

ITRE rapporteur Gregorová's draft reports on CSA2 and the NIS2 amendments presented 8 Oct 2026 remain in the amendment phase (PE796.470/.484/.485/.501 tabled 13 Oct).

**Implementation agenda.**
- **24 / 27** Member States have a NIS2 transposition law in force as of 4 Nov 2026. Most recent to apply: Austria (NISG 2026, 1 Oct 2026), Netherlands (Cbw, 15 Aug 2026), Luxembourg (law of 5 May 2026). ([ClevSec](https://clevsec.one/blog/nis2-transposition-status/)).
- **Still outstanding:** France (Loi Résilience withdrawn from Assemblée floor sessions of 7 and 9 Oct 2026; no new date in the Conférence des présidents conclusions of 13/20 Oct 2026); Ireland (National Cyber Security Bill not yet introduced in the Oireachtas); Spain (Cortes dissolved by RD 806/2026 of 6 Oct 2026; general election 23 Dec 2026).
- **CJEU cases still active:** INFR(2024)0270 (ES), INFR(2024)0274 (FR), INFR(2024)0279 (IE). Dutch case INFR(2024)0272 closed 1 Oct 2026. ([directive-nis2.eu](https://directive-nis2.eu/blog/netherlands-nis2-court-case-closed)).
- **Near-term pivots:** NIS Cooperation Group plenary 26 Nov 2026 (Irish Presidency); national PQC roadmaps due under the NIS CG Coordinated Implementation Roadmap by 31 Dec 2026.

**Organizational impact.**
- **Essential/important entities** in FR, IE, ES: continue operating under the old NIS1 regime but prepare for retroactive scope-sweep once transposition laws take effect; penalty regimes will apply from day one of national application.
- **Cross-border groups** that currently rely on NIS1 jurisdiction in those three states should keep NIS2-aligned registers and incident procedures ready to switch on at short notice.
- **Dutch entities:** the Cbw has been applicable for ~12 weeks; monitor BT/BZK sectoral guidance as implementing decrees land.

### 2. DORA (In force)

**Discussion.** No new ESA or Joint Committee publication this week. The 16 Sep 2026 *DORA Incident Reporting Operational Instructions* and the 18 Sep 2026 EBA/GL/2026/09 *Guidelines on the sound management of third-party risk* (non-ICT counterpart to DORA) remain the controlling texts ([MoFo Q3-2026](https://www.mofo.com/resources/insights/261102-european-digital-compliance-key-digital-regulation)). Oversight of the 19 designated CTPPs continues; horizontal reviews of contracts and SLAs under EBA Lead Overseer are reported to be ongoing.

**Implementation agenda.**
- **First full oversight cycle** for the 19 CTPPs designated on 18 Nov 2025 progresses — expect direct engagement, horizontal contract reviews and risk-based on-site inspections through Q4 2026 and into 2027; ESAs are also running the **re-designation data collection** for the 2027 list.
- **Digital Omnibus (Art. 8 of the Council mandate)** would rewrite only the first subparagraphs of DORA Art. 19(1)/(2) — a proportionality fix to the incident notification cascade; trilogue mandate from Parliament still needed before Q1–Q2 2027 negotiations. ([regreportingdesk.com](https://regreportingdesk.com/digital-omnibus-incident-reporting-national-entry-point/)).
- **Register-of-Information** second cycle acceptance (94 % in 2026 vs. 40 % in 2025) sets the baseline for the 2027 CTPP list review.

**Organizational impact.**
- **Significant banks** should have completed 31 Oct 2026 DORA-aligned ICT concentration disclosures; next focus: evidence of threat-led penetration testing (TLPT) coverage of critical-or-important functions ahead of ECB-led deep-dives.
- **CTPPs:** budget for Joint Examination Team requests through 2027 and clarify sub-contracting chains documented in the Register of Information.
- **Non-ICT third-party providers to banks** now fall under EBA/GL/2026/09 — firms need to extend DORA contractual playbooks to non-ICT suppliers from 2027.

### 3. eIDAS 2.0 / EUDI Wallet (In force / Guidance)

Topic AA (Secure Cryptographic Application / SCA) refinement iteration 7 remains open (paper v0.8 of 14 Oct 2026; closes 16 Dec 2026). The EUDI Wallet certification pathway via FCAF at [conformance.eudi.dev](https://conformance.eudi.dev) continues to be populated.

**Implementation agenda.**
- Implementing acts in force since July 2026: CIR 2026/1730, 2026/1731, 2026/1735 (amending CIR 2024/2977 PID/EAA, 2024/2979 integrity & core, 2024/2982 protocols) ([ARF v3.0.0 release notes](https://github.com/eu-digital-identity-wallet/eudi-doc-architecture-and-reference-framework/releases/tag/v3.0.0)).
- **Operational milestone:** national wallet solution rollouts targeted through 2026 toward eIDAS2 obligation that each Member State issues at least one wallet; FCAF conformance test suites are the gate before national certification.

**Organizational impact.**

- **Qualified trust service providers / Relying Parties:** Trusted-Lists + LoTEs (ETSI TS 119 612 / 119 602) are now dual-source requirements under ARF 3; extend trust-anchor retrieval pipelines accordingly.
- **Online services in regulated sectors** (banks, healthcare, public) should begin Relying Party registration planning under CIR 2025/848 even where national supervisory bodies have not yet notified operational portals.

### 4. Cybersecurity Act v2 (Proposed)

**Discussion.** This week's pivot is in Parliament: **IMCO vote on its opinion on CSA2 is scheduled for Tue 10 Nov 2026 09:30 (Week 46 agenda item 12)** per the European Parliament Legislative Observatory and epvotes.com ([2026/0011(COD) dossier](https://epvotes.com/dossiers/2026-0011-european-union-agency-cybersecurity-enisa-european)). A Council working party met on **5 Nov 2026**. ITRE amendments PE796.470/.484/.485/.501 tabled 13 Oct 2026 are now circulating with sectoral reactions: Centr notes DNS-security-related ITRE amendments tasking ENISA to assist Member States with DNSSEC and DNS-attack mitigation, plus FOSS-sensitive language in the trusted ICT supply-chain framework ([Centr EU Policy Update, Oct 2026](https://www.centr.org/policy/policy-updates/item/eu-policy-update---october-2026.html)). ITRE committee adoption remains expected in Q1 2027; law-firm consensus for plenary / Council adoption is 2027 at the earliest.

**Implementation agenda.** No binding deadlines until adoption. Expected post-adoption framework: (i) new ENISA mandate (early alerts, ransomware recovery support, vulnerability management, single reporting entry point per Digital Omnibus); (ii) ECCF streamlining — scheme development within 12 months by default, managed by ENISA; (iii) trusted ICT supply-chain framework with Commission implementing acts for key ICT assets and high-risk suppliers; **mobile networks phase-out: up to 36 months**; (iv) fine ceilings of 7 % / 2 % / 1 % of worldwide annual turnover depending on the category of breach.

**Organizational impact.**
- **Electronic-communications networks operators (mobile, fixed, satellite):** CSA2 is self-executing against high-risk vendor components — initiate inventory of 5G/IP-core supplier dependencies **now**.
- **NIS2 essential/important entities** outside telecoms: no direct new obligation today, but a Commission implementing act under CSA2 could designate ICT assets as key and require supplier-level risk assessment.
- **Certification bodies / scheme owners:** anticipate accelerated scheme pipelines (EUMSS, EU5G, EUCC v2) with 12-month windows.

### 5. Quantum / Post-Quantum Cryptography (Guidance)

**Discussion.** No new ENISA PQC publication this week. The Germany BSI/BMDS/BMI joint national PQC guideline of 12 Oct 2026 remains the latest major national benchmark; the ECCG Agreed Cryptographic Mechanisms (ACM) v3 draft (2 Jun 2026, consultation closed end-Jul 2026) remains under revision — applicable version is still ACM v2 of 6 May 2025 ([EUCC Cryptography guidelines](https://certification.enisa.europa.eu/publications/eucc-guidelines-cryptography_en)). The NIS CG workstream on PQC continues to prepare its second deliverable supplementing the June 2025 Coordinated Implementation Roadmap.

**Implementation agenda.**
- **31 Dec 2026 — National PQC roadmaps due** from every Member State ([EC digital-strategy PQC page](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/post-quantum-cryptography)). At under eight weeks out, delivery risk is elevated for Member States that have not yet published a draft.
- **21 Dec 2030 — High-risk use cases** (energy, water, finance, health, transport critical infrastructure) migrated; quantum-safe software/firmware upgrades enabled by default from that date.
- **31 Dec 2035 — All use cases** migrated.

**Organizational impact.**
- **Essential entities under NIS2 and CRA manufacturers:** begin PQC inventories now; expect ACM v3 (hybrid KEM/signature guidance) to become reference by Q1 2027 for EUCC certification scopes.
- **Trust service providers:** plan dual-stack (hybrid PQ/T) deployment; qualified-signature/seal migration paths depend on ETSI algorithm catalogue updates.
- **SMEs:** at minimum, capture cryptographic bill-of-materials now, before CRA's December 2027 substantive obligations amplify documentation requirements.

## Cross-cutting themes

- **Live-fire crisis coordination.** The refrigeration-systems incident is the first visible test this year of the joint stack — EU-CyCLONe + CSIRTs Network + Europol EC3 + ENISA + DG CNECT — reporting out in parallel with CRA Art. 14 reporting flowing into the new SRP. Expect this incident to feature in the 10 Nov Lepassaar ITRE presentation and to reinforce the political case for the Digital Omnibus's harmonised incident-reporting rewrite.
- **Horizontal supply-chain governance converges.** CSA2's trusted ICT supply-chain framework, CRA's manufacturer obligations, NIS2 Art. 21(2)(d) supply-chain measures, EBA/GL/2026/09 non-ICT third-party guidelines and the Data Act cloud SCCs now form a layered — and partially overlapping — stack. Legal/procurement playbooks need an orchestration layer rather than per-regime templates.
- **Product-security operational layer is maturing faster than law.** SRP v1.3, ENISA's Secure Update advisory and the EUCC CSP guideline all landed before Germany's CRA implementing law is promulgated — operational reality is leading national transposition for product manufacturers.
- **Transposition divergence stabilising at 3.** The remaining NIS2 outliers (FR, IE, ES) share a single root cause — unresolved national politics — not substantive EU-law disagreement. Risk that their eventual laws diverge from the harmonised baseline grows as the Parliament debates amendments to NIS2.
- **PQC deadline cliff is now ~7 weeks away** for Member State roadmaps. Operational silence from most Member States this week increases execution risk.

3. **PQC roadmap stocktake.** For entities headquartered in a Member State that has not yet published a national PQC roadmap, prepare a one-page gap note for executive review before the 31 Dec 2026 deadline.

### Compliance — 30 days
1. **NIS2 FR / IE / ES contingency.** Freeze a NIS2-ready compliance pack for group entities in the three outstanding Member States so that activation lead-time is < 60 days from local promulgation.
2. **CRA Art. 14 reporting matrix.** Confirm thresholds, roles and timelines (24 h early warning / 72 h update / 14-day vulnerability final / 1-month severe incident final) are mirrored in the incident playbook.
3. **DORA cross-walk with EBA/GL/2026/09.** For credit institutions and PIs/EMIs: extend the Register of Information scope-note to cover non-ICT third-party arrangements; pre-write contract amendment templates.

### Legal — 90 days
1. **Cloud SCC adoption.** Review existing cloud-service master agreements against Commission Recommendation 2026/2394; identify where the SCCs can be adopted verbatim and where carve-outs are required for DORA/NIS2 security clauses.
2. **CSA2 scenario planning.** For telecoms clients, model a worst-case 36-month phase-out of high-risk vendor components in mobile cores; capture breakage clauses in supplier agreements.
3. **EUDI relying-party registration.** Prepare the legal basis / terms-of-use for Relying Party Services under ARF v3.0.0 Section 3.11.2, including trust-anchor retrieval and user consent texts.

### Risk & third-party — 90 days

2. **Supply-chain harmonisation layer.** Build a single supplier risk register that satisfies NIS2 Art. 21(2)(d), DORA contract-register requirements, CRA Art. 13 SBOM expectations and emerging CSA2 trusted-supplier assessments in one dataset.
3. **AI-model security watch.** Track Commission follow-up on the October RFI responses from GPAI providers; prepare a position on Article 15 cybersecurity obligations for internal AI pipelines.
